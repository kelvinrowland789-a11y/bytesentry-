Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain language, how ByteSentry uses information needed to provide the service.

Information used

When you register or use ByteSentry, we handle account details such as your name, phone number, email, password and transaction PIN (stored as protected hashes), wallet balance, recipient numbers, and purchase or payment records.

How information is used

  • To authenticate your account and protect access.
  • To process wallet funding and the data or airtime orders you request.
  • To display balances, subscriptions, order status, and transaction history.
  • To investigate support requests, errors, or suspected misuse.

Service providers

Information required to complete a transaction, such as the recipient number and selected service, may be shared with the relevant data, airtime, or payment provider. Those providers process information under their own practices.

Security and retention

We use safeguards such as protected password and PIN hashes and authenticated sessions. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Transaction history can be removed from the history view; removing an activity does not reverse a payment, wallet ledger entry, or completed delivery.

Your choices and contact

Keep your account details current and contact support@bytesentry.ng with privacy questions or account concerns.
