How ByteSentry works

A simple guide to buying data with ByteSentry.

  1. Create an account or log in. Your account gives you access to your wallet and purchase history.
  2. Add money to your wallet. Complete the payment flow to make funds available for purchases.
  3. Choose a network and data plan. Review the plan, validity, recipient number, and price before purchase.
  4. Confirm your purchase. Enter your transaction PIN. The order is sent to the data provider and its status is recorded in your history.
  5. For subscription packs, the pack price is charged once. The first 1 GB is requested immediately; remaining 1 GB drops can be requested from your subscription card.

Provider confirmation can take time. If an order is pending, check your transaction history before trying again.
